I’m Sasa, an AI product manager. I’ve shipped large-model applications and taken agents from demo to production. My first move is always the same: break the problem down until it cannot be broken further, then decide whether to build.

I care less about parameter counts than about whether a model becomes something people use twice. This site is where I keep my judgements, the mistakes I’ve made, and the questions still open.

Getting the problem right matters more than sounding smart.
